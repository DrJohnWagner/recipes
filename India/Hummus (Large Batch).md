# Instant Pot Hummus (Large Batch)

**Prep Time:** 25 mins
**Cook Time:** 17 mins
**Natural Release:** 10 mins
**Total Time:** About 1 hr
**Servings:** 48 servings
**Calories:** 106 kcal per serving

Use your Instant Pot to make light and creamy hummus from split, dehusked Bengal gram with no soaking required.

## Ingredients

### CHANA DAL:

* 1 litre chana dal, rinsed
* 6 litres filtered water

### HUMMUS:

* 12 cups cooked chana dal, still warm
* 2 cups warm cooking liquid
* 1 cup tahini
* 8 medium cloves garlic
* 4 large lemons, juiced
* 4 teaspoons kosher salt
* 6 teaspoons ground cumin
* 4 teaspoons smoked paprika
* 1 cup extra virgin olive oil, highest quality

## Steps

### TO COOK THE CHANA DAL:

1. Rinse the chana dal thoroughly and discard any debris.
2. Place the chana dal and filtered water in the Instant Pot insert.
3. Close the lid, make sure the vent is set to "sealed" and cook on high pressure for 17 minutes.
4. Allow the pressure to release naturally for 10 minutes, then carefully release the remaining pressure.
5. Drain the chana dal, reserving 2 cups of the cooking liquid.

### HUMMUS:

1. Add 1 cup of the reserved cooking liquid, tahini, garlic, lemon juice, salt, cumin, smoked paprika and olive oil directly to the warm chana dal in the Instant Pot liner.
2. Blend with an immersion blender, adding additional reserved cooking liquid as needed, until the hummus is smooth, creamy and almost whipped.
3. Taste and adjust the salt, lemon juice or cooking liquid as needed.
4. Serve topped with za’atar, smoked paprika and a splash of olive oil.

## Notes

* The chana dal should be cooked until very soft and beginning to break down.
* Blending directly in the Instant Pot liner makes cleanup extremely quick.
* Portion any remaining cooked chana dal and cooking liquid into containers and freeze them for future batches.
* Slightly warm refrigerated or frozen chana dal and its liquid before blending.
* Store prepared hummus in sealed containers in the refrigerator for up to five days.
